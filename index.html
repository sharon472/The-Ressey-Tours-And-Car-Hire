Have Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you hit the road.

What is the minimum age to hire a vehicle?
You must be at least 23 years old with a valid driver's license to hire any vehicle from us.
Do I need driving experience to rent a car?
Yes. We require at least 2 years of licensed driving experience for all self-drive bookings.
What documents do I need?
You'll need a valid driver's license, national ID or passport, and a payment method (M-Pesa, card or cash accepted).
Is there a deposit required when booking?
A refundable security deposit may be required depending on the vehicle selected. It is returned once the car is safely returned in good condition.
Can I take the car outside Nairobi on long trips?
Yes! Our vehicles are available for upcountry and cross-country travel across Kenya. Some routes may carry a small additional fee — let us know when booking.
What happens if I return the car late?
Late returns may attract an additional hourly or daily charge. Please contact us in advance if you need to extend — we're always happy to help.
Do the rental cars come with insurance?
Yes. All our vehicles include comprehensive insurance coverage as part of the hire price for complete peace of mind.
How can I make a reservation?
Book online via our Hire a Car page, WhatsApp us at +254 727 347 926, or call us directly. We confirm every booking within 1 hour.
